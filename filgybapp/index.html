A little more detail.

Does FilGyb connect to my bank?

FilGyb does not require your bank login. Automatic capture uses the supported alerts you pass through an iOS Shortcut. Setup is required, and compatibility varies by alert format.

What happens to messages and recordings?

Selected bank messages and recordings are sent securely to our services for analysis. Financial records are stored locally on your device. Read the Privacy Policy for processing and retention details.

Can I use different currencies?

Each account keeps its own currency. Combined totals use approximate exchange rates when available. These may differ from your bank’s final rate.

Is a subscription required?

FilGyb offers a monthly essentials plan and FilGyb Pro, an annual plan with expanded features and allowances. Apple shows your localized price and any eligible offer before purchase. Subscriptions renew unless canceled in Apple Settings.
