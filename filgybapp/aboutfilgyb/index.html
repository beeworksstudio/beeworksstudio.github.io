A NOTE ABOUT FilGyb

Why we built FilGyb

We built FilGyb for anyone who wants to see this month’s spending alongside the bills coming next.

Everyday purchases. The bill coming next week. A goal that matters. An account in another currency. FilGyb brings the details into a considered, simple view so you can understand where you stand.

Make an entry yourself, record it with your voice, or configure supported bank-message automations. Keep track of budgets, subscriptions, installments, rent, savings goals and the things you own or owe.

Designed for iPhone with English and Arabic layouts, FilGyb pairs a restrained interface with practical financial organization. It helps you keep records; it does not access your bank login, execute payments or guarantee savings.
